## Material list notes

**Polished Diorite**:
- Since your average concrete roof is 4-6 inches thick, we'll count each block as 1/4 of a cubic meter.
- *5774.25 cubic meters* of concrete
	- 23097 original blocks

**Polished Andesite:**
- Since commercial concrete flooring is ~6-8 inches, so we'll count each block as 3/8 of a cubic meter of concrete
- *2279* cubic meters of concrete
	- From 6078 base blocks
**Brick**:
- Assuming that it's brick veneer, and not actually a meter thick of brick
- Brick veneer is typically 1 inch thick
- Exterior walls are typically 8-10 inches thick
	- Assuming concrete as well, just look at the inside of the building
- 0.009 cubic inches of brick
	- 25,200 kg
	- 27.77 tons
	- 5767 original blocks
**Glass**:
- 3863 original blocks
- Residential windows are around 3-6mm
- 3.864 cubic meters of glass
	- 9660kg of glass
**Gray Concrete**:
- 1482 original blocks
- Support walls are typically 10-12 inches thick
- 444.6 cubic meters
**Smooth Stone**:
- 867 original blocks
- Patio floorings are 4 inches thick on average, I'm saying 5 inches since it has extra tables and heating elements on it.
- 110.109 cubic meters of concrete
**White Concrete**:
- 425 original blocks

- 63.75 cubic meters of concrete
**Smooth Sandstone**:
