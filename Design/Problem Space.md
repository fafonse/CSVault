## Different Approaches

- **Reductionist Approach**
	- *Scientific*
	- Break complex systems into parts and examining them seperately
	- Generates *truth and rules* (binary)
- **System Approach**
	- *Holistic*
	- Examine the entire system to find relationships, patterns, and connections
	- Generates *options and possibilities* (spectrum)
