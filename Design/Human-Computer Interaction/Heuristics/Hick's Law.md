Heuristic for determining the time for a user to make a decision out of a whole list.
$$ T = b * log_2(n + 1) $$

| Variable | Meaning                 |
| -------- | ----------------------- |
| T        | Time to take a decision |
| B        | Submenus/total menus    |
| n        | Amount of decisions     |
- Time increases with # of options
- Only applicable for simple decisions
- Oversimplifying
- Useful for quick, mindless, option selection
- Not widely applicable to today's HCI
