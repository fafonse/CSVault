The process of testing your system/software/design with real users.
	
> Remember to state the purpose of your study from the beginning

## Prep
How do you select people for a user test? 

- Select people representative of target population
	- Minimize friends/family
	- Job specific vocabulary/knowledge
	- Approximate, if needed
		- Students in the field, apprentices, etc
	- Use incentives to get participants
		- Gift cards, free shit, etc.
- Prepare tasks typical of actual product use

**Practice to avoid "bugs"**
## Team Roles
Usually, you're going to have to juggle multiple hats, but if you have the money, hire people.
- Greeter
	- Puts users at ease and gets demographic data
- Facilitator
	- Only team member who speaks
	- Gives instructions, encourages thinking out loud
- "Computer"
	- Simulate feedback and response
- Observers
	- Take notes and recommendations

## Conducting the Test
Remember to respect your tester's time and patience.

- 30-60 minute session max
	- Preparation + test + debrief
- Record the data
	- Your memory isn't that good
	- Camera, video, audio
	- **Take explicit consent**
- Describe the *purpose of the study*
### Ethical Considerations
We have to be respectful of our testers, and we cannot force them to do anything they don't want.

- Tests can sometimes be distressing
- You have a responsibility to alleviate distress
	- Make voluntary informed consent
	- Avoid pressuring testers
	- Let tester's know they can leave at any time
	- Stress you are testing your system, not their abilities
	- *Anonymize collected data*
- Often must get human subjects approval (IRB)
