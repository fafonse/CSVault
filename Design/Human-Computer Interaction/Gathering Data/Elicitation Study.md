Getting users to provide the answer for you.

> Given *x* action, which interaction would a user intuitively do?

**Pros**:
- Often simpler, more diverse, and better quality ideas
- Doesn't need a lot of participants (~7-10)
**Cons**:
- Large variety of responses, *low agreement*
- People may be biased by previous experiences
- People are not designers, ideas may be bad
> [!example] Touch Gestures
> How would users try to close a window with touch gestures?
> *Some examples users come up with:*
> - 5 Finger pinch
> - 3 Finger drag from top of screen
> - Wipe away
