Predicts time required to move a pointer through a "tunnel".

> How long would it take to move a pointer through the *Scary Maze Game*?


- Time depends on length and width of the tunnel
- Independently discovered and studied 3 times

## Formula
$$ MT = a + b * (A / W)$$
$$MT = a + b * \int_c ds / W(s)$$
Parameters:
- MT
	- Movement Time
