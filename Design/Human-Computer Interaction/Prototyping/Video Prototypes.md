Capturing the prototype's intended usage and experience on *film*. Meant to be a minimal viable product, not [[Prototyping#High fidelity|hifi]] or anything.

> If a feature isn't important enough for a video, it might not be necessary for a first version of the product.

- Cheap and fast
- Great communication tool
- Captures use context
- Portable and self-explanatory
- Regular people "get it"
- Videos last forever, and old code breaks

## Methods
What do I do to be as lazy as possible??

- You can do a "stop motion" like video, where it's closer to an animatic than a film
- Photoshop UI and elements over real screens in your video
	- Use paper prototypes instead of screens/photoshop if you're more low fidelity
- Use a voice over to contextualize the situation and explain user actions

## Dos and Don'ts

Don'ts:
- Don't say "our app"
- Don't do developer interviews, this is a *showcase*, not a pitch
Do's:
- Use photos/videos of real people and places
- People should act out "roles" in the video
- Ground interactions in real-life context
	- Give the video a *situation* that the project could be used in
- Use dialogue without narration if you can make it obvious whats going on.
