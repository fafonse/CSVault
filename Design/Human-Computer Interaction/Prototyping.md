Creating a usable version of a product that you can test with users.

Prototyping will help you find problems earlier, faster, and more intuitively. 

## Fidelity
The level of detail in your prototypes.

### Low Fidelity
- Usually done on paper	
- Low detail, sketch like

You can create fast iterations, test multiple easily, fake almost all interaction. Super cheap way to prototype, no equipment really needed.
Also **non-programmers can participate**.

*However...*
- Hard / impossible to implement some functionality
	- UI animations/feedback
- Won't look or feel like the final product
	- High suspension of disbelief
- End users can't use it themselves
- Can't show to clients

### High fidelity
- Looks like the final product
- Designed with digital tools

You can show it to clients, and you get to play around with feedback/animations that can only be done in digital.

*However...*
- Hi-fi tools encourage more precision
	- scope creep
- Tester/Reviewer perception warped
	- Representation communicates "finished"
	- Comments focus on nonfunctional details
	- "When can we have it?"
