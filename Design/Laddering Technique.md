Laddering is a method to help elicit the higher or lower abstractions of the concepts that people use to organize their world.

- **Encourage stories over short answers**
	- Can you tell me about a time when...
- **Ask open ended questions**
	- How do you feel about this?
- **Don't lead the witness!**
	- Don't ask questions leading in a bias
- **Ask why, especially for the obvious stuff!**
	- Ask even if the answer seems obvious
- **Don't be afraid of silence**
		- Hold it longer than your comfort level, let them think
