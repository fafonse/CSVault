## Commuter Survey Report
- Over half of all respondents travel 8 mile or less to campus destinations
	- Majority can either walk/bike one way
	- Less than half use their cars, even less use if they're a student
	- Faculty are the main car drivers
- Commuters describe their commute primarily through *parking*
	- It's expensive to own a parking pass
- Majority of commuters come from South and surrounding areas of the University
- Commuting is often multi-modal and relies on a bunch of complex reasons
	- Weather, time of day, extra errands, storage, etc.
- Students actually produce less greenhouse gas than the staff

## Increased Bicycle Use

What if scenario of a typical European city switching to majority bike transportation

- 77% of all transportation now is by private car

What if:
- Car owners think 22% of all km could be replaced with bikes or other micro mobility
- Estimated 500 tons of reduced CO2
- ~1.4 square km could be saved for green space instead of parking
- 8 million tons of waste reduced from manufacturing new cars
	- 40 billion liters of water saved annually
- Drivers would save 25 hours annually from not being in traffic
	- GDP increase by 0.2% !!
- Drivers save 1,330 euros annually

However, micro mobility is not just limited to bikes, but facilities must be made for e-scooters and ride share programs as well. Cities also need to be safer, since fatalities would double if we just increased bike usage.
