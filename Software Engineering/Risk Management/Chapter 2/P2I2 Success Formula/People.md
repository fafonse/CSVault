The human element in managing [[Software Risk|risk]].


> *There is no substitute for people. Only people make decisions; without them risk management would no happen.*

**Key Points**
- Involve people at all levels of risk management
- Education/training/experience contribute to risk management ability
- Motivation must be sufficient to overcome the barriers of adopting something new
- Individuals have risk preferences you can use to predict their behavior
## Involving People at All Levels

Typically we focus on improving technologies and automation, but we rarely actually train people and coordinate for risk management.
People at all levels can contribute by managing risk within their respective fields (management, [[Work|work]], engineering, etc.). 

- Management
	- Functional management
	- Senior management
	- Board members
- Engineers
- Technical Leads
- And yes, even *customers*
	- Sponsors/investors
	- Clients/users

## Developing Risk Management Ability
Begin by learning that risk is *inherent in all large software systems*, and increased complexity also means increased risk. When people know risk is present, they can actually begin to [[Discover|search for it]] and deal with it. 

- Education helps us to gain the basics we need to reason about risks.
- Training gives people the vocabulary to communicate risk
	- Doubles as team building
	- Specialized risk management should be reserved to those who actually work with it

## Motivation
Changing people and education can take weeks or months, but changing strategy and culture can take *years*.
Use a [[Force Field Analysis]] to determine the risks with adopting new practices, and ideally make it a [[Best Practice]] within your organization.
