A land feature on a solid surface.
## Structural
Formed from magma and rocks through *tectonic processes*.
> Mountains, rift valleys, fault scraps

## Weathering
Formed from weathered rocks and debris.
> Soils, karst caves, etc.

## Erosional
Formed from the transportation of debris by wind/water/ice.
> River and glacial valleys, gullies, etc.

## Depositional
Formed from deposition due to wind/water/ice.
> Sand bars, alluvial fans, deltas, floodplains, glacial tilt, etc.
