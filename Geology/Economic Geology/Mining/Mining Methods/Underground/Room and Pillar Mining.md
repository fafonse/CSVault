As you mine, you leave pillars behind to hold up your mine walls and such. The economics/extraction ratio is determined by how big your pillars are.
The fundamental method of mining underground.

- Done with continuous miners
- Pillars begin to curve as they take on *abundant pressure*
- Typically used for a stratified, weak tabular deposit of ore.
- Cheap at first, but operationcal costs don't scale with size well
- Lots of logistical and timing optimization required

Typically *shuttle cars* help move ore, which can increase costs in the long term. The conveyor belt that was 20 ft away in the beginning of mining can go to 200ft away.

	At the end of the mine's life, you can start *robbing pillars*. This is when you extract the pillars and let the mine collapse. Can be dangerous.
