Using water jets to knock ore loose, then you can filter the ore out of the sludge.

> An early mining technique, not really used now (at least for gold) 

## History
- After the California Gold rush, miners needed a way to extract the more elusive gold deposits
- Use water to blast old river banks, and use long *sleuces* that would capture and filter the heavy gold out of the lighter waste
- The tailings caused the river level to rise significantly, as mines dumped their waste into local rivers. This caused local flooding, mud, and river traffic.
- Regulation caused mines to become abandoned and deserted.
