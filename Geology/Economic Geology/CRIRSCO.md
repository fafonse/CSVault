A group of representatives of organizations that are responsible for developing mineral reporting codes and guidelines. Combined capital in this group accounts for >80% of the listed capital of the mining industry.

Groups:
- Australasia (JORC)
- Brazil (CBRR)
- Canada (CIM)
- Chile (National Comittee)
- China (CAMRA)
- Colombia (KAZRC)
- Mongolia (MPIGM)
- Philippines (PMRCC)
- Russia (OERN)
