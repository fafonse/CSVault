## Grade Types
Mine engineers work with a lot of unknowns. The grade system is the bare minimum of material classification. 

- *Cutoff*
	- Minimum grade that differentiates [[Ore|ore]] from *waste*
- *In-Situ*
	- The grade from a sample of the deposit
	- A single point sample in the wider mine
- *Diluted / Mine grade*
	- The in-situ grade mixed with unavoidable waste that will be mined with it. Will almost always be lower than the in-situ grade.
- *Head*
	- Grade of material that is fed into mill/plant/processing
- *Recovered/Product grade*
	- The grade of material that leaves the mill/plant/processing circuit
