This plan provides the general skills needed to manage risk. The levels will bring you from a novice to an expert in *risk management*. 

1.  *Understand where your goal is*
	- Learn how to find the direction to your goal when you can't see it
2. *Know your capability with tools*
	- *A fool with a tool is still a fool*
3. *Determine the dimensions of pitfalls*
	- Learn how to avoid and analyze large hazards.
	- *A high project cost indicates an unsuccessful risk manager*
4. *Use the right tool*
5. *Aim toward the goal*
	- Position yourself to be successful before action
6. *Focus on the task at hand*
	- Not on the risk or goal
7. *Commit to It*
	- Practice is needed to get better
8. *Follow through*
9. *Assess for Improvement*
10. *Take corrective action as required*
	- After you modify your strategy, begin again at step one.
