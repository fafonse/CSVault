An individual's behavior with respect to risk for each discipline in the [[Six-Discipline Model|6-D Model]].

**Scoring**:
- Give yourself 1-5 points depending on the practice you perform consistently.
- Team score is the average of the independent scores

**Grading**:
- < 6
	- Read *Thriving on Chaos*
- 7 $\rightarrow$ 12
	- Awareness makes you uncomfortable
	- It gets better
- 13 $\rightarrow$ 18
	- More proactive than most
	- An asset to the team
- 19 $\rightarrow$ 24
	- More quantitative than most
	- Asset to *any team*
- 25 $\rightarrow$ 30
	- Self-motivated and great attitude
