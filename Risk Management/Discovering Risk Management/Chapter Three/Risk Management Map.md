A map that charts the course for growing your capability to manage [[Software Risk|software risk]].

- Following the map at the right pace will give you a reasonable path for progress.
- The map survives obstacles like staff turnover and project cancellation

**Five Stages**:
- [[Problem stage|Problem]] 
- Mitigation 
- Prevention 
- Anticipation 
- Opportunity
**Avoids several problems**:
- Laying a *spotty* foundation
- Jerky progress and adoption
- Veering off course



## Map Format

- Circles/Nodes denote *stages*
- Arrows represent *transitions*
	- Always take you to a higher risk management capability
	- Three parts
		- *Vision*
			- Ideal state
		- *Goals*
			- Define where you should be
		- *Strategy*
			- Specific approach to goals
