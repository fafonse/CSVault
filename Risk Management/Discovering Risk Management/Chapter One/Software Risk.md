 A measure of the likelihood of bad things happening to your software project
 
- Best to start with your project goals and uncertainties and then work backwards
	- Quantify your losses, risk, and uncertainties

> *We make no progress without conquering risk*
## Categories
- Project Risk
	- Primarily a management responsibility
	- EX: Resource constraints, external interfaces, supplier relationships
	- Hard to predict due to reliance on external factors
- Process Risk
	- For management, like HR responsibilities and project management
	- For technical, engineering process (design, requirements, testing)
- Product Risk
	- Technical responsibility
	- Stability, design performance, code complexity, test specifications
