Closed loop approach for process optimization. Each part is a [[Discipline|discipline]], each must be mastered and practiced on their own a bit.

This process can be applied to your personal life as well, and makes the *human brain* an *ultimate enigma*, as it's the most perplexing computer we have.

1. [[Envision]] the product
2. [[Plan]] the work
3. [[Work]] the plan
4. [[Measure]] the work
5. [[Improve]] the process
6. [[Discover]] the possibilities

> The last two steps were created by the [[Elaine M. Hall|author]], and are an extension of the work of *Edwards Deming*
