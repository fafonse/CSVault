> *Success begins with a high-quality plan.*

The best plan is one that is...

- **Proactive**
	- Take the action to *prevent problems*
	- Getting a *independent facilitator* can help you find [[Motivation|motivation]] or when you have hard time [[Implementation, Project Execution|implementing]] by yourself
		- *Lack of time*, *low risk assesement cofidence*, *low comfortability discussing risk*
- **Integrated**
	- Implemented as part of the production process
	- *Not more or less important than our work, but rather, a part of the remaining task*
- **Systematic**
	- Follow through with your risk resolution
	- More risk management compared to risk identification
		- *20% risk assessment, 80% risk control*
- **[[Six-Discipline Model|Disciplined]]**
