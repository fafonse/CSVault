The organizational foundation that is needed to establish a [[Motivation|risk-aware culture]].

**Key Points**
- Senior manager values  [[Policy to Affect Behavior|affect workers through policy]]
- Customers and senior managers [[Set Expectations with Requirements|set expectations for project behavior through requirements]]
- Resolve risks through the [[Applying Resources|application of resources]]
- Determine the business case through [[Risk Management Costs and Benefits|costs and benefit analysis]]


**Four Infrastructure Factors**
- *Organization*
	- Creating a proper environment that supports effective working relations
- *Requirements*
	- Minimum standards for projects
- *Resources*
	- The investment required to manage risk
- *Results*
	- The return on investment for managing risk
