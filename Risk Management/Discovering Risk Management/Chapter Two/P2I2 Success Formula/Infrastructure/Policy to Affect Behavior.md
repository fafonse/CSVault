Company culture is a reflection of senior management values, which are documented in [[Policy|policies]] that affect behavior.

Once a risk management policy is in place, risk-aware culture can be developed in several ways.
- Top-down
	- Managers provide leadership and resources foaffect workers through *risk management*
- Bottom-up
	- Empowerment of the workforce and rewards for positive results
- Sideways
	- Definition of roles and responsibilities in the organization chart
