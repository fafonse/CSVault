A technique to help people understand the positive and negative aspects of change.

- Create a table showing *Driving Forces* vs. *Restraining Forces*
- *Driving forces* are your [[People#Motivation|motivation]] for overcoming *restraining forces*


| Driving Forces                           | Restraining Forces             |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Provides a focus on goals                | Management lacks commitment    |
| Satisfies customer requirement           | People are resistant to change |
| Increases visibility for high-risk areas | Team has no time for training  |
> Driving forces provide reasons for risk management adoption, restraining forces are the barriers to it.
