**Key Points**:
- There are five process elements in the *risk management process*
- The standard process is not one size fits all
	- The defined process should be flexible
	- Different strategies for managing risk can be effective at different levels of scale
- Process execution must be cost-effective
	- Too much will cost more than the actual risk

## Five Essential Elements

1. [[Risk Identification Process|Identify]]
	- Identifying [[Risk|risk]] and sources of risk.
	- People do better framing risk as "unknowns" rather than stating them outright
	- "What are your assumptions?" is better than "What are your risks?"
- *Analyze*
	- Analyzing risk based on established criteria
	- Estimate probability of risk, consequence of risk, how risks can create new problems together
- *Plan*
	- Plan the next task to resolve a risk
	- Develop risk action plans, alternative strategies
	- Established thresholds determine when to execute a plan
- *Track*
	- Monitoring planned thresholds and risk status
	- Use triggers to give early warnings
	- Report [[Measure|measures and metrics]]
- *Resolve*
	- Respond to warnings, execute a plan, report the results
