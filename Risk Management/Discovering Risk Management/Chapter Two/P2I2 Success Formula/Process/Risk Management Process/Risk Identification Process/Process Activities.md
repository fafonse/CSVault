[[Activities]] of the [[Risk Identification Process]].

1. Conduct a [[Risk Assessment|risk assessment]]
2. Identify risk systematically
	- Several categories
		- *Checklist, interview, meeting, review, routine input, survey, working group*
3. Define the risk attributes
	- Identify the *probability* and *consequences* of your risk
4. Document the identified risk
	- Use a [[Risk Statement|risk statement]]
5. Communicate the identified risk
