An ordering, but no implication of equal distance between the different points of the scale.

> [!example]
> - Educational level (high school, some college, degree, graduate)
> - [[Likert Scale]] of 1-5
> - Social class (lower middle, upper)
> - Ticket class on the Titanic (1st, 2nd, 3rd)
> - Class standing (freshman, sophomore, junior, senior)
