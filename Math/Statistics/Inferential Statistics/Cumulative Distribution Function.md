Probability of a certain outcome that falls within the cumulative possible answers.

- Starts at 0, and always ends up at 1
- For a dice roll, your $CDF(2)$ would be 1/6, since on average, you got a 1/6 chance of rolling a 2

> Works by finding the area under a curve from $(-\infty, x)$. To find a smaller section, we would just do $F(-\infty, b) - F(-\infty, a)$ to find the area under the section $(a, b)$
